# Lexi Money

The money section of Lexi: a free, debt-clearing-focused view of income, bills, spending and debts.

## Language

**Safe to spend**:
What is left of a month's income once bills, the yearly set-aside, debt payments, the buffer and any recovery payment are covered.
_Avoid_: Disposable income, free cash

**Living expenses**:
All spending that is not a bill payment.
_Avoid_: Day-to-day spending, variable spending

**Spending limit**:
A monthly cap the user sets on living expenses in one category, watched alongside safe to spend rather than taken out of it.
_Avoid_: Budget, envelope, allowance

**Pace**:
How much of a spending limit would be used by today if it were spread evenly over the month.
_Avoid_: Run rate, burn rate
