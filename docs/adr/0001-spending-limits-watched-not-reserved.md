# Spending limits are watched, not reserved out of safe to spend

Spending limits on living expenses sit alongside safe to spend as caps to track against; they are not subtracted from income the way bills, the yearly set-aside and debt payments are. Reserving them would change the safe-to-spend formula that the Overview, the Plan, what-if projections and the Money MCP tools all share, and would bring back the envelope bookkeeping removed in the October 2026 rebuild. Going over a limit only warns: nothing is blocked, nothing has to be covered, and unspent limit does not roll over.

## Considered Options

- **Reserve limits out of safe to spend** (envelope style): safe to spend would become "money for everything else". Rejected for the reasons above.
- **Watch limits as caps** (chosen): safe to spend is unchanged; a quiet note appears when limits add up to more than safe to spend.
